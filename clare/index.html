The story

12 years in the making

I was VP of Merchandising in New York, working on handbags for Kate Spade, Tory Burch and Marc Jacobs. I know what makes a bag sell, and what makes one worth keeping.

Now I'm a new mom in Toronto, and I couldn't find the bag this chapter called for. So I'm designing it, and sharing every step along the way.
